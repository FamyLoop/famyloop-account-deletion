Delete your FamyLoop account

FamyLoop by Fuyama Hong Kong Limited. Last updated: 18 October 2026

You can delete your FamyLoop account and the data stored for it at any time. Choose whichever way suits you.

Option 1: delete it in the app

This is the fastest way and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open FamyLoop and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and scroll to the bottom, to the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

Option 2: ask us by email

Use this if you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in.

  1. Send an email from the address you signed up with to the address below.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my FamyLoop account.
  3. We may reply to confirm that the request is really from you.

Email famyloop@gmail.com

We aim to delete the account within 30 days of confirming the request.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and sign-in account (email address, display name, account type, personal code).
  • Your cloud backup: daily check-ins, medications and reminder responses, brain-game statistics, to-dos, and your saved daily tip.
  • The invites and nudges you sent or received.
  • Your shared copy in any family group. You are removed from the group, and the administrators can no longer see your data.
  • If you delete in the app, the FamyLoop data on your phone as well.

Before you delete

  • Subscriptions are not cancelled automatically. Cancel any paid plan in Google Play (Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions), or Google will keep charging you.
  • If you created a family group that still has other members, transfer ownership to another administrator or delete the group first. The app asks you to do this.
  • Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore an account after it has been deleted.

What may remain

  • Purchase records held by Google Play, which Google manages under its own policies.
  • If a group’s creator deletes the whole group, each other member’s shared copy is removed the next time that member opens the app.
  • Technical logs or backups kept for a limited time by our infrastructure providers (Google and Cloudflare).

Delete only some of your data

You can delete individual check-ins, medications and to-dos inside the app. To delete a whole category at once without closing your account, email us from your account address and say what you would like removed.

More detail on how we handle data is in our Privacy Policy.
